## Colored noise for sleep, relaxation, and focus

Different noise colors distribute sound energy across frequencies in different ways. This gives each one a distinct character:

- **Brown noise** has a deep, low-frequency sound.
- **Pink noise** has a balanced, softer character.
- **White noise** spreads energy evenly across frequencies.
- **Blue noise** emphasizes higher frequencies.
- **Purple noise** has an even brighter high-frequency character.

Lullwave also includes mixes of noise waves and noise pulses, making it easy to find a background sound that suits your environment.

## Ambient and nature sound library

These sounds can provide a consistent audio backdrop for winding down, reading, working, or masking distracting environmental noise.
