# App Screenshot Creator

Create a consistent set of screenshots for the App Store or Google Play with descriptions, custom layouts, and connected backgrounds. Editing and export happen locally in your browser.

## Supported features

- **Screenshot input** - add up to 10 PNG, JPEG, or WebP images, with a limit of 25 MB per image.
- **Output dimensions** - choose Android phone, iPhone, or iPad presets, swap to landscape, or enter custom dimensions.
- **Frame management** - reorder, replace, or remove screenshots in the sequence.
- **Descriptions and typography** - customize text, font, size, weight, alignment, and color for each frame.
- **Composition controls** - adjust margins, text area, text placement, and status-bar cropping; add an outline or drop shadow.
- **Backgrounds** - use solid colors, individual frame gradients, or one continuous gradient across the entire set.
- **Shared or individual settings** - apply composition, typography, and backgrounds across the set or override them for a selected frame.
- **Text fit checks** - identify overflowing descriptions before downloading.
- **Image export** - save the selected frame as PNG or JPEG, or download all frames as numbered images in a ZIP.
- **Local processing** - images and descriptions stay in the current tab; download your work before refreshing or leaving.
