# Crop, Trim, and Resize Videos Online

Crop, trim, resize, and convert videos without uploading them. Adjust the framing, duration, resolution, and output quality directly in your browser.

## Supported features

- **Video input** - open MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM, MKV, or AVI files when their codecs are supported by your browser and device.
- **Flexible cropping** - move and resize a crop selection, or position and zoom the video inside a fixed output frame.
- **Fit to frame** - keep the complete video visible with black bars when the source and output aspect ratios differ.
- **Video trimming** - select a time range with trim handles, exact timestamps, or the current playhead.
- **Selection preview** - preview the selected time range and crop before exporting.
- **Aspect ratios and resolution** - choose common landscape, square, or vertical ratios; keep the crop resolution, select a long-edge preset, or enter custom dimensions.
- **Output controls** - set the frame rate and adjust quality to control the video bitrate.
- **Video export** - save as WebM, MKV, or MP4 when the required encoders are available. Audio is preserved when supported; a warning appears if export must omit it.
- **Local processing** - edit and export on your device. Export requires WebCodecs over HTTPS; recent Chrome and Edge offer the best compatibility.
