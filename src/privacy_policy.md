# Privacy Policy

Curved Nebula respects your privacy.

We do not collect, store, or share your personal information.

However, our apps use the **[Google AdMob](https://admob.google.com)** service, which may use your approximate device location. This information allows advertising providers to deliver advertisements they believe are relevant to you.
