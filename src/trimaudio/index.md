# Trim Audio Online

Trim and convert audio using an interactive waveform. Preview the part you want to keep, choose your output settings, and save it locally without uploading your file.

## Supported features

- **Audio input** - open MP3, WAV/WAVE, M4A, Ogg/OGA, Opus, FLAC, or AAC files when your browser can play and decode their codecs.
- **Interactive waveform** - click or drag to move the playhead and navigate through the audio.
- **Precise trimming** - drag the selection handles or enter exact start and end timestamps.
- **Selection preview** - listen to the selected range before exporting.
- **Audio conversion** - export uncompressed 16-bit WAV, or Ogg Opus, M4A AAC, and MP3 when the browser provides the required encoder. Unsupported compressed formats are disabled.
- **Bitrate selection** - choose an output bitrate for compressed audio.
- **Stereo to mono** - optionally mix stereo audio down to a single channel during export.
- **Local processing** - keep your audio on your device. Export requires WebCodecs over HTTPS; recent Chrome and Edge offer the best compatibility.
