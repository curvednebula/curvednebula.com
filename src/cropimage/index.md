# Crop and Resize Images Online

Crop and resize photos, prepare app icons, or create avatars at exact pixel dimensions. Everything happens locally in your browser, so your images are never uploaded.

## Supported features

- **Crop shapes** - choose square corners, round, squircle, or rounded corners with a custom radius.
- **Aspect ratios** - use a preset or adjust freeform crop proportions; round and squircle crops use a 1:1 ratio.
- **Position and zoom** - drag, use arrow keys, scroll, or pinch to frame your image precisely.
- **Rotate and flip** - rotate in 90-degree steps and flip horizontally or vertically.
- **Exact output size** - resize to a custom width and height, with an optional aspect ratio lock.
- **Image export** - save as PNG, JPEG, or WebP, with adjustable quality for JPEG and WebP.
- **Transparent shapes** - export round, squircle, and rounded-corner crops as transparent PNGs.
- **Local processing** - edit and download images directly on your device.
