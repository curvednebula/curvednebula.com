# Lullwave - Noises and Sounds for Sleep and Relaxation

(opens new window)

# Overview

Lullwave is an Android app for sleep and relaxation, offering calming sounds and a full range of colored noises: white, pink, brown, purple, and more.

(opens new window)

No registration, no subscription. Sleep, relax, mask tinnitus with colored noises, noise mixes, and ambient nature sounds:

  • Brown noise
  • Pink noise
  • White noise
  • Blue noise
  • Purple noise
  • Mix of noise waves and noise pulses
  • Water streams
  • Rains
  • Sea waves
  • Water droplets
  • Boiling water
  • Train
  • Fireplace
